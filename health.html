Safety & Transparency
What Nexus Medical City is, and is not
Purpose

To help people decide what to do next about a health concern (self-care, pharmacy, a doctor, or emergency care) and to keep their own health information organised. Nexus Intelligence is a technology assistant. It is not a doctor, does not diagnose, and does not prescribe.

Safety design
  • Emergency first. Typed warning signs (chest pain, breathing difficulty, stroke signs, severe bleeding, self-harm and others) trigger an emergency banner and a one-tap call button before any AI reply.
  • Local emergency numbers for your country, always with a "numbers may change" notice. Nexus does not dispatch ambulances.
  • Clear disclaimers before first use and inside the chat.
  • No invented data. Doctor profiles are labelled as samples until real, verified doctors join. No payment is taken.
Known limitations
  • AI replies can be wrong or incomplete. Never delay care because of an AI reply.
  • Warning-sign detection is keyword-based, so it can miss unusual wording. If you are worried, seek care.
  • Language support beyond English has not been clinically reviewed.
  • Reminders work while Nexus is open on your device.
Clinical review & compliance status

The triage guidance has not yet been independently reviewed by a clinical advisory board, and Nexus holds no medical-device certification. Planned next steps: clinician review of the red-flag rules and prompts, privacy compliance review (NDPR and GDPR principles), and clinical validation before any claim of accuracy is made.

Privacy & Data
You are in control
Where your data lives
  • On this device only: vitals, medications, health records, medical ID, family profiles, appointments.
  • In your Nexus account: your Nexus Intelligence chats, so you can continue them later.
  • Sent for analysis: text, photos and documents you choose to send to Nexus Intelligence are processed by our server to produce a reply.
  • On our server (only if you turn on background reminders): reminder times and your notification token. Medicine names are never sent.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS. We do not sell your health data.
